Who We Are

Welcome to Kalyani Agro Foods, your reliable source for top-quality Indian agricultural and food products. Located in India and established by Mrs. Rupali Karle, the company is known for its excellence in international exports. Committed to quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction, we supply global markets with premium pulses, eggs, fruits, and vegetables.
